This is a linear regression based ML model used to predict the prices of used cars based on the following factors:
1. Brand
2. Model 
3. Year of manufacturing
4. Age of the car
5. Distance Driven in km
6. Transmission (manual or automatic)
7. Owner (first or second)
8. Fuel Type
9. Posted Date

Source of Data: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/mohitkumar282/used-car-dataset

The data has 9582 rows. For the training of the model we will consider 80% of the data (7666 instances) and the remaining 20%
(1916 instances) will be used for testing of the model.

Since Posted Date for most of the cars is nov-24 it is not a good measure so we will drop it.

The dataset does not contain any missing values so we don't have to worry about those.

The askedPrice column is in string format we also need to convert it to integer format.
